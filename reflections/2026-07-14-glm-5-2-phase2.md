## On the work done

The highest-value judgment was the **ESC9 gate set: FN-safe subset only.** The
review (phase 1) flagged ESC9 as inconsistent with ESC1/ESC10/ESC14. The clearly-
correct, FN-safe fix is enroll + manager-approval (these can only suppress
templates that are not attacker-reachable). The EKU and enforcement gates are
genuinely ambiguous — adding them risks a false negative (worse for a defensive
tool) or suppresses ESC9 on most exports. So I implemented what's unambiguously
right and documented the rest. This is the project's own discipline: close the
clear gap, track the ambiguous one.

The **publication-prep gotcha was real and nearly missed.** The canonical gate
(denylist = the concatenated work-domain token) passed, and it would have been
easy to declare clean and flip. The looser search for the spaced form (the form
the user explicitly named) caught a two-comment leak in the pre-commit hook. The
lesson reinforced: the gate matches the concatenated token; the *human-readable
spaced form* is a different string and must be searched for separately. The
skill's gotcha #5 ("don't name the forbidden token in the gate's own files") is
exactly this — and the fix ("describe it as 'the work-domain token'") is what I
applied.
